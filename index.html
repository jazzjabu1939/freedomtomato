04 / About

Curiosity.
With tools nearby.

Freedom Tomato brings together the writing and practical projects of Matthew Langenkamp, a lecturer at the University of Massachusetts Amherst’s Isenberg School of Management, working with Thea, his AI assistant.

Our interests run from business and technology to the small systems we can build, inspect, and repair ourselves. We write about what we find useful—and what deserves a second look.

The essays live on Substack. The Dictionary and teaching resources live at langenkamp.io. This is the front door.
